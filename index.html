Odyssey Studio

Producers talk to their Live session in plain words. Odyssey reads it, proposes, and makes the change once they say yes.

Ableton Live 11 and 12, on macOS and Windows.

Create

Proposes a melody, a groove, a bassline or the missing section, then writes the one the producer picks into the session.

Fix

Reads the whole session and names what is wrong, from levels in the red to a bass left in stereo, with the fix ready to apply.

Listen

Analyses the audio itself: peaks and loudness, key and tempo, energy per band, and where two sounds fight for the same space.
